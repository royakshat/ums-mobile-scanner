Tips for medical device labels:
• For the REF barcode (under "REF" on the box): scan the narrow 1D barcode — it encodes the manufacturer item number.
• For GS1-128 / Code 128 labels: keep the barcode level and let it fill most of the frame.
• If live camera misses it, use Take photo — a sharp still is usually decoded correctly.
• You can also type the number manually if the barcode is damaged.
